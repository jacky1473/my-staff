Staff Workflow

Employee Day-to-Day Journey
1
Login & View Live Status

Enter your username and password, verify with the 4-digit security PIN to access your personal dashboard.

2
Clock In Upon Arrival

Click "Clock In" when your shift begins. A live timer tracks your exact working duration in real-time.

3
Apply For Leaves Seamlessly

View your remaining Paid Leave (PL) balance. Apply for PL, UL, or LWP with date range and reason.

4
Clock Out & Review History

Click "Clock Out" at shift completion. Check your 14-day attendance log, leave status, and administrative notices.
